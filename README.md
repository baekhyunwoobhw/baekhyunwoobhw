My academic journey began with a deeply personal mission: to understand and help my sister, who has dyslexia. This drive took me all the way to the University of Oxford, where I studied medicine, psychology, and psychiatry. However, after years in labs and lecture halls, I realized that textbook theories cannot truly cure or capture human psychology. Realizing that formal education falls short of genuine understanding, I dropped out of my PhD (DPhil) program at Oxford and returned home to Jeju, South Korea.

Today, I have stepped away from traditional academia to embrace life on my home island. I have proudly taken over my mother’s legacy as a Haenyeo (Jeju free diver). While my sister now works at a florist in South Korea and spends her free time creating art, I am focusing my energy on the local community. My mission is to use my unique background to support and empower young people—from elementary, middle, to high school students—right here in Jeju, helping them thrive outside the constraints of traditional expectations.

Beyond the ocean and my community work, I am an adventurer at heart. The thrill of the open road on my motorcycle, the challenge of conquering new climbing routes, and the peace of camping in the great outdoors are essential parts of who I am. These pursuits keep me grounded and connected to the raw beauty of the world.
